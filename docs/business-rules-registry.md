Updated: 24 November 2026
Purpose: **Single source of truth** reconciling conflicts across `OCEC-Portal-Requirements.md`, `docs/testing/test-strategy.md`, and `docs/backend/production-runbook.md`. Whenever an older document disagrees with this registry, **this registry wins**.
